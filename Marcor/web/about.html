ABOUT MICHAEL DELL

The man behind the machine

Michael Dell didn't just build a computer company — he revolutionized an entire industry. From a small dorm room at the University of Texas at Austin, he built what would become one of the largest technology corporations in the world.

Michael Saul Dell was born on February 23, 1965, in Houston, Texas. He is the founder, chairman, and CEO of Dell Technologies, one of the world's largest technology infrastructure companies. His journey from a college student selling computer parts out of his dorm room to leading a $100+ billion global enterprise is a testament to entrepreneurial vision, relentless innovation, and strategic leadership.

Early Life & Background

Born February 23, 1965, in Houston, Texas. His father, Alexander Dell, was an orthodontist, and his mother, Lorraine Charlotte, was a stockbroker. From a young age, Michael showed an extraordinary entrepreneurial spirit — at just 12 years old, he earned $2,000 selling stamps through a mail-order business. He attended Memorial High School in Houston, where he demonstrated an early aptitude for business and technology.

Personal Life & Family

Michael Dell married Susan Lieberman in 1989. Susan, now known as Susan Dell, is a fashion designer and philanthropist. Together they have four children and reside in Austin, Texas. Despite his remarkable success, Michael Dell is known for maintaining a relatively private lifestyle. He is an avid technology enthusiast, a licensed pilot, and a dedicated philanthropist committed to education and children's health initiatives through the Michael & Susan Dell Foundation.
